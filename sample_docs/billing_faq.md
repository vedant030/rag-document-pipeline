# Billing FAQ

Invoices are generated on the first day of each month and emailed to the billing contact. Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date. Customers on annual plans receive one invoice per year.

You can upgrade your plan at any time and the change takes effect immediately, with the price difference prorated for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

Refunds are available within 14 days of the first purchase of a plan if you are not satisfied. After that window, charges are non-refundable, except where required by law.

If a payment fails we retry three times over seven days and email the billing contact each time. If all retries fail the workspace moves to read-only mode until the balance is paid.
